\begin{abstract}
We propose SDACS, a hybrid airspace control system that fuses
short-horizon Artificial Potential Field (APF) avoidance with
medium-horizon Conflict-Based Search (CBS) planning under a unified
5-layer safety net. A wind-aware mode switching mechanism automatically
adapts APF parameters when wind speeds exceed 10\,m/s, while CBS
re-planning is triggered upon persistent APF conflict states. On
10-scenario benchmarks with 5 random seeds against ORCA, Velocity
Obstacle, and CBS baselines, SDACS reduces near-miss rate (NMR) by
55\% versus VO, increases airspace utilization (AU) by 14\% versus
ORCA, and sustains real-time factor (RTF) of 140$\times$ even in
1000-drone scenarios. Code, benchmark datasets (CC-BY-4.0), and
reproducibility container are publicly released.
\end{abstract}

\section{INTRODUCTION}

The expected scale of Urban Air Mobility (UAM) — up to 100,000 drones
operating concurrently by 2030~\cite{kopardekar2017utm} — requires
airspace control systems that simultaneously guarantee safety, achieve
high throughput, and remain robust under environmental disturbances.
Existing approaches fall into two categories:

\textit{Reactive avoidance} (ORCA~\cite{vandenberg2011orca}, Velocity
Obstacle~\cite{fiorini1998vo}) responds quickly but loses up to 30\%
safety margin under wind speeds above 10\,m/s. \textit{Centralized
planning} (CBS~\cite{sharon2015cbs}) provides global optimality but
scales as $O(N^2)$ in agent count, making real-time deployment
challenging for fleets exceeding 100 drones.

This paper presents SDACS, which makes three contributions:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item A wind-aware hybrid avoidance combining APF (10\,Hz) with
          CBS re-planning (1\,Hz, triggered).
    \item A 5-layer safety net providing fault isolation across drone
          agent, APF, CPA prediction, ATC controller, and UTM
          integration layers.
    \item An open benchmark dataset of 10 scenarios with 3 baselines,
          and a Docker-based reproducibility package.
\end{enumerate}

\section{METHOD}

\subsection{5-Layer Safety Net Architecture}

The SDACS architecture (Fig.~\ref{fig:arch}) decomposes airspace
control into five layers, each with independent failure modes:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Layer 1: Drone agent (SimPy 10\,Hz)
    \item Layer 2: APF avoidance with wind-aware parameters
    \item Layer 3: Closest Point of Approach (CPA) 90\,s lookahead
    \item Layer 4: ATC controller (1\,Hz, CBS re-planning)
    \item Layer 5: UTM integration (K-UTM, ADS-B, Remote ID)
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Wind-Aware APF Mode Switching}

\begin{equation}
\boldsymbol{\theta}_{\mathrm{APF}}(t) =
\begin{cases}
\boldsymbol{\theta}_{\mathrm{default}} & \text{if } v_{\mathrm{wind}}(t) \le 10 \, \mathrm{m/s} \\
\boldsymbol{\theta}_{\mathrm{windy}}   & \text{if } v_{\mathrm{wind}}(t) > 10 \, \mathrm{m/s}
\end{cases}
\end{equation}

\subsection{CBS Re-planning Trigger}

\begin{algorithm}
\caption{CBS Re-planning Trigger}
\begin{algorithmic}
\STATE $N_{\text{persistent}} \leftarrow 0$
\WHILE{simulation running}
    \IF{APF reports conflict at time $t$}
        \STATE $N_{\text{persistent}} \leftarrow N_{\text{persistent}} + 1$
    \ELSE
        \STATE $N_{\text{persistent}} \leftarrow 0$
    \ENDIF
    \IF{$N_{\text{persistent}} \ge N_{\mathrm{threshold}}$}
        \STATE Invoke CBS replan; reset counter
    \ENDIF
\ENDWHILE
\end{algorithmic}
\end{algorithm}
