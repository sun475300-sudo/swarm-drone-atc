SDACS (군집드론 공역통제 자동화 시스템) 배포 완료!

서비스 접근 방법:

1. 백엔드 API:
   kubectl port-forward svc/{{ include "sdacs.fullname" . }}-backend 8080:8080

2. 대시보드:
   kubectl port-forward svc/{{ include "sdacs.fullname" . }}-dashboard 3000:3000

3. Redis:
   kubectl port-forward svc/{{ include "sdacs.fullname" . }}-redis 6379:6379
